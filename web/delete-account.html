Delete your VehicleHub account and data

VehicleHub — vehicle service, fuel and document tracker

Option 1: In the app (immediate)

  1. Open VehicleHub and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings (or tap your photo at the top to open Profile).
  3. Tap Delete Account and confirm with your password (or your Google account).

Your account and all of its data are deleted straight away.

Option 2: By email (if you no longer have the app)

Send an email to contact@zecodeek-it.com from the email address you signed up with, with the subject “Delete my VehicleHub account”.

We will confirm the request and delete your account and data within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account (email, name and profile photo)
  • All your vehicles, service records, fuel logs, documents and service schedules
  • All photos you uploaded (vehicle, receipt and document photos)
  • Reminders scheduled on your phone

What may be kept

Nothing from your account is kept after deletion, except where the law requires us to keep a record. Anonymous, aggregated usage statistics that cannot identify you are not affected.
